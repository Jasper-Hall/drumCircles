drum-circles euclidean rhythm, extended
rheome.tools

tap a genre to load it. tap it again for a variation.

Every track is a euclidean rhythm: pulses spread as evenly as possible across steps, turned by rotation. Distribution is the extension: it slides the pulses toward the front or the back of the bar, which reaches grooves a plain euclidean sequencer can't, like the tresillo under reggaeton and dancehall. Made by Rheome.
